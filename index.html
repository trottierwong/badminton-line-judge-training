Badminton Line Judge Training

Interactive volunteer training • Approximately 5–10 minutes

Welcome, volunteers!

Learn to judge whether a shuttle lands IN or OUT, use clear hand signals, and handle situations where you cannot see the landing.

Key rule: A shuttle touching any part of the relevant boundary line is IN. Judge where the shuttle first contacts the floor, not where it rolls or where feathers extend.

Watch your assigned line. Call promptly and impartially. If you genuinely cannot see the landing, do not guess.

Lesson 1 — Line judge signals

These illustrations show the hands and arm positions. Official line judges should follow the referee's briefing and current BWF instructions.

IN

Point your right hand toward the line. No verbal call.

OUT

Call “OUT!” clearly and extend both arms horizontally with open hands.

UNSIGHTED

Cover your eyes with both hands if your view of the landing is blocked.

Lesson 2 — Know the boundary

Singles: The long, narrow court. Doubles: The wider court. The back boundary for doubles service is different from the back boundary during a rally.

For every call, use the boundary relevant to the event and phase of play. If unsure which line you are assigned, ask the umpire or referee before play begins.
